## Alerts

```````````````````````````````` example tree
> [!NOTE]
> Read the *guide*.
.
document [1:1-2:19]
  alert [1:1-2:19] type="note"
    paragraph [2:3-2:19]
      text "Read the "
      emph
        text "guide"
      text "."
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example tree
> [!tip]
> One.
>
> Two.
.
document [1:1-4:6]
  alert [1:1-4:6] type="tip"
    paragraph [2:3-2:6]
      text "One."
    paragraph [4:3-4:6]
      text "Two."
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example tree
> [!FOO]
> Text.
.
document [1:1-2:7]
  block_quote [1:1-2:7]
    paragraph [1:3-2:7]
      text "[!FOO]"
      softbreak
      text "Text."
````````````````````````````````


